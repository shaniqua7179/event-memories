# 04 · Guest and admin journeys

Status: **draft for review** (2026-09-22)

The audience includes church family and older guests on phones. Every screen uses plain words, big buttons, one clear next step, and a help phone number.

## Guest journeys

### G1 · Scan and share (the primary journey)
SCAN → EXPERIENCE → EXPLORE → REMEMBER → UPLOAD → SHARE → KEEP EXPLORING

1. **Scan** the QR code at the venue, on a table card, or in a text. The link opens `/event/epps60th`; no app.
2. **Experience** (first 3 seconds): the disco ball turns behind the title, light glints across the screen, and a few guest photos drift into view. The headline and honoree animate in. A sticky **"Share a memory"** button is always within thumb reach.
3. **Explore:** scrolling brings the living wall, featured moments, and a few guestbook cards.
4. **Remember:** tap any photo to open it large, swipe through, read the caption, play videos.
5. **Upload:** tap **Share a memory**.
   1. **Choose photos & videos**, which opens the camera roll directly. Previews appear with their sizes checked.
   2. **Your name** (remembered for next time).
   3. **Email (optional)**, "only if you'd like us to send you the album link."
   4. **Add a few words (optional):** a caption for the photos.
   5. **Leave a message for Pastor Epps (optional):** a birthday wish or memory.
   6. **Sharing choices:** ☑ Show in the event gallery · ☑ Others may download my photos (both on by default, with one-line explanations).
   7. **Share**, with per-file progress, retry, and a screen kept awake.
6. **Share (confirmation):** a short celebratory moment (light burst, then a thank-you). It says exactly what was received and what happens next, and offers "Share more," "See the wall," and "Send this page to a friend."
7. **Keep exploring:** the guest returns to the wall. In auto mode, their photos are already there.

### G2 · Just browsing (after the event)
Opens a link from a text or Facebook → the hero and wall → gallery filters (Latest · Featured · Videos · Collections) → lightbox → downloads where allowed → full-screen slideshow → guestbook.

### G3 · Message only
"Share a memory" → "Just leave a message" → name, message, and type (wish / memory / thanks / funny) → confirmation.

### G4 · Couldn't attend
Same as G1/G3. The copy explicitly welcomes "celebrating from afar."

### G5 · Something went wrong
- **File too big or wrong type:** a friendly reason on that tile; the rest continue.
- **Signal dropped:** tiles show "Didn't send · Tap to retry"; unfinished uploads resume on return.
- **Uploads closed:** "Sharing has closed. Thank you! Everything is still here to enjoy."
- **Still stuck:** "Call 972.230.4477 and we'll walk you through it," or email the photos.

### G6 · "Please remove my photo"
Each photo has a small "Is this you? Ask us to remove it" link. It sends the organizer a request with the photo code, so the item appears flagged in the admin review queue.

## Organizer (admin) journeys

### A1 · Sign in
Footer **Admin** link or `/admin` → password (email remembered) → the events list, or straight to the event if they only manage one.

### A2 · Review what's new (the most common task)
The review queue shows the oldest first, one item at a time and big:
- **Approve (A)** · **Hide (H)** · **Reject (R)** · **Feature (F)** · **Crop & rotate (C)** · **Skip (S)**
- Photos from the same submission can be approved together ("Approve all 12 from Denise").
- A badge shows the count waiting, and new arrivals appear live.

### A3 · Find and manage content
Media and Messages pages: search by name, caption, or photo code; filter by status, uploader, date, and type; sort by newest, oldest, or taken-at. Select many to approve, hide, feature, download (ZIP), or delete. Change a photo's caption or download permission.

### A4 · Run the TV slideshow
Settings → **Open live wall** → the `/live` URL opens full screen on the TV. It rotates approved photos, videos, and messages; new approvals slide in. Pause, skip, and speed controls stay hidden until the mouse moves.

### A5 · Configure the event
Open/close uploads (or schedule a close); moderation mode (approval / auto); allow photos, videos, messages, comments; global download switch; limits; wall style; slideshow speed.

### A6 · Brand the event
Name, honoree, date, headline, welcome message, upload instructions, colors, fonts, logo, hero video and poster, hashtags, help contact, link-preview image. A live preview shows changes before saving. A QR code for the event link can be downloaded as a PNG for print.

### A7 · Check the numbers
Photos, videos, and messages by status; contributors; uploads over time; storage used against the plan limit (with a warning at 80%).

### A8 · After the event
Switch to **archived**: uploads close automatically, and the site becomes a permanent, read-only memory book. Download everything as a ZIP for the family.

### A9 · Set up the next event (platform admin)
Create event → slug → settings from a template (birthday, wedding, gala…) → branding → invite moderators → share the QR code.
